Data & Configuration Suite

For the people who own master data and SAP configuration — data governance, data quality and master data leads.

3 modules · SAP-native · ECC and S/4HANA
Module 1 of 3
DataLift
Measures master data quality against rules your business defines, scores it by object and by plant, and trends it over time. Quality becomes a number you manage, not an opinion.
Explore DataLift →
Module 2 of 3
Customizing X-Ray
Checks configuration against the master data actually in use and surfaces settings that no longer resolve: deleted groups, orphaned keys, values that nothing points at any more.
Explore Customizing X-Ray →
Module 3 of 3
Production Readiness
Validates production-critical data and its dependencies before an order runs, so a missing inspection plan or an incomplete BOM surfaces in planning rather than on the shop floor.
Explore Production Readiness →
